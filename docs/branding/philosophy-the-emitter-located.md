# The Emitter, Located — ewcalc

**The house.** OldCrow's repos share one drafting table: hairline
measurement accumulated until a shape emerges, an Italiana wordmark over a
mono whisper, and one accent spent only on what has been fixed and named.
The libraries' plates belong to an imaginary field guide (corvid
cartography, figs. 1–3a). ewcalc is an application, not a specimen — same
hand, different notebook. No bird; a new plate series in roman numerals.

**The figure.** The EW bench drawn as its own discipline. A directional
antenna pattern — main lobe on boresight, decaying sidelobes, a small
backlobe — accumulated from radial hairlines against dB rings and bearing
ticks. Three dashed DF bearings (the sensor's boresight and two remote
stations) converge on a single point wearing its error ellipse. Along the
bottom, an intercepted pulse train annotated τ and PRI.

**The gold.** In this discipline the accent's meaning is domain-native: an
emitter location is called a fix. The gold marks the fix and nothing else —
the one thing the calculator computed.

**Two grounds.** The master is the house ink (`#161b23`) with cream
linework (`#e7e0cf`). The light variant re-derives both sides instead of
inverting: the ground is the ink's own hue tinted toward white
(`#dde4ef`), the linework is the gold family deepened to a dark
goldenrod-brown (`#5f4a1d`), and the true gold (`#c8a24e`) stays on the
fix alone, brighter and warmer than everything around it.

**The caption.** `plate i — the emitter, located`.
